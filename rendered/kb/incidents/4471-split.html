The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. A tenant whose locale writes the day first gets day and month swapped: “Booked for 2 July. The invite says 7 February.” This is the bug #4471 names, and the queued fix is for it. Every tenant on file is day first.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells people to type the month first. Its date field prints MM/DD/YYYY. People at a day-first tenant on booking-v3 type what the hint says, and the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first. Same swap, different bug: the parser fix does not touch the form’s hint. Only reports sent through the form, at a booking-v3 tenant, are this.

day-shift: the date moves by one day. These dates are not swapped. “Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting.” An invite that “arrived before I made the booking”. The day depends on where the reader is: a time zone bug, not a format bug. The cause table counts these under parser and footnotes them; the parser fix will not fix them.

How three became one

  • Decision 0001 merges on the subject line and nobody reads the body. Every report here has a subject that says “wrong day” in some form, so all three problems went into one ticket. The decision planned for one bug under different words; it did not plan for different bugs under the same words.
  • Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser. The cause depends on the tenant and the way in (causes.ts). The two disagree, so the tag cannot split the ticket.
  • The export repeats reports across its pages. Count by id.

What the desk does now

  • Keep #4471 for parser only. Move hint and day-shift reports to the tickets above.
  • When a report says “one day off”, read it before you merge it: that is day-shift.
  • Re-read triage: it was last reviewed in 2024.
