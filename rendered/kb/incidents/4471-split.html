The three problems

parser: the shared parser read every date month first. A tenant whose locale writes the day first got day and month swapped: “Booked for 2 July. The invite says 7 February.” This is the bug #4471 names. Fixed on 5 October: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.

hint: the booking-v3 form told people to type the month first. Its date field printed MM/DD/YYYY. People at a day-first tenant on booking-v3 typed what the hint said, and the form read it the tenant’s way, day first. Same swap, different bug: the parser fix does not touch the form. Only reports sent through the form, at a booking-v3 tenant, are this. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired.

day-shift: the date moves by one day. These dates are not swapped. “Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting.” An invite that “arrived before I made the booking”. The day depends on where the reader is: a time zone bug, not a format bug. The parser fix does not fix it, and the engineering update does not name it. Open. The cause table files these reports under parser and only footnotes them, so they closed with #4471.

How three became one

  • Decision 0001 merges on the subject line and nobody reads the body. Every report here has a subject that says “wrong day” in some form, so all three problems went into one ticket. The decision planned for one bug under different words; it did not plan for different bugs under the same words.
  • Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser. The cause depends on the tenant and the way in (causes.ts). The two disagree, so the tag cannot split the ticket.
  • The export repeats reports across its pages. Count by id.
